Finish the Dossier

A last pass over your own analysis before you hand it in. It asks the questions; the answers are in your movement, your composition book and your readings.

There are no answers on this page, and that is not me being difficult.

Every dossier in the class analyses a different movement. Nobody else claimed yours, so no page could tell you which joints are in it, which muscles do the work, or what the lever class is — those depend entirely on the movement you picked. What this page can do is ask you the things a reader will ask, in the order they will ask them, so you find the gaps before someone else does.

If you need the content — what a condyloid joint is, which contraction is which — that is the Unit 1 review, your readings and the book. This page is only about whether your analysis of your movement is finished.
