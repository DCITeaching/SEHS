The vocabulary each layer expects. Open the layer you are writing, or search. The names are not the task — choosing the right ones is.

Why a word list does not finish the dossier

Your data booklet is open in every exam you sit, and the IB's own note on Paper 1A says that because of it there is less reliance on recall of the terminology for anatomy. The words are not what is being tested.

So this page will not tell you which joints are in your movement, which muscle is the agonist in your return phase, or what your lever class is. Those change with the movement you claimed, and choosing them is the dossier. Dossier Q used real anatomical terms throughout and still scored badly.
